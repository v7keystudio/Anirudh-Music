COMPOSER · PRODUCER · PERFORMER

ANIRUDH RAVICHANDER

“The Sound Behind Modern Indian Cinema” — a generation's heartbeat, set to music.

Anirudh Ravichander is a Chennai-born composer, singer and music producer whose work has redefined the sound of Tamil cinema and travelled far beyond it. From the viral phenomenon of "Why This Kolaveri Di" in 2012 to the seismic, genre-bending scores of "Master," "Beast," "Jailer," and "Leo," his music blends folk textures, electronic production and orchestral scale into something instantly recognisable as his own. Over a decade-plus career he has scored some of the biggest blockbusters in Indian cinema, working alongside its most iconic stars and directors.

01 — Biography

A composer who became a generation's sound.

"Music isn't background — it's the emotion the audience didn't know they needed yet."

— ANIRUDH RAVICHANDER
Birth
Born 16 August 1990 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, into a family deeply rooted in Carnatic music tradition.
Family
Grandson of veteran composer R. Vaidyanathan; grew up surrounded by classical training and film-music heritage.
Education
Studied at Padma Seshadri Bala Bhavan, Chennai, before pursuing formal training in sound engineering and music production.
Profession
Film composer, playback singer, music producer and live performer — one of the most in-demand voices in contemporary Indian cinema.
Journey
Began as an assistant to composer Harris Jayaraj, absorbing the discipline of large-scale film scoring before striking out independently.
Breakthrough
The 2012 single "Why This Kolaveri Di" became a cultural phenomenon overnight, announcing a singular new voice in Indian pop.
Style
A signature fusion of folk percussion, electronic synthesis and orchestral grandeur — equally at home in a stadium anthem or an intimate ballad.
Achievements
Multiple Filmfare, SIIMA and Vijay Awards, sold-out world tours, and a discography that defines the modern sound of Tamil, Telugu and Hindi cinema.
